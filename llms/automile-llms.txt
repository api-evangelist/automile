# Automile

> Automile is a fleet-telematics platform: a plug-and-play GPS "Automile Box" plus web/mobile apps that track vehicles in real time, log mileage automatically, and manage drivers, trips, geofences, expenses, inspections and work orders. Serving 15,000+ businesses. The REST/JSON API at api.automile.com (OAuth2) exposes the full fleet surface plus a publish/subscribe webhook stream.

## APIs

- [Automile API](https://api.automile.com): REST/JSON fleet-telematics API, Swagger 2.0, /v1, OAuth2 (read/write). 454 operations across vehicles, trips, contacts, geofences, triggers, vehicle health, expenses, inspections, work orders, and publish/subscribe webhooks.
